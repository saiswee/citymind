# Pitch Video Script (5:00 target)

Read this roughly as written — it's timed to land near 5 minutes at a normal speaking pace.
Bracketed notes tell you what to show on screen; they're not spoken.

---

**[0:00–0:25] — Hook + problem**
[Show: a real photo of Hyderabad flooding, or the Overview map]

"In Yousufguda and Erragadda, Hyderabad, the same two junctions flood every monsoon. It's
happened in 2018, 2024, and again this year. Residents know it. Shopkeepers know it. But
none of that knowledge lives anywhere a municipal team could act on — it's scattered across
WhatsApp groups and memory. That's the gap CityMind closes."

**[0:25–0:35] — Introduce CityMind**
[Show: Dashboard]

"CityMind is an AI workspace that turns a real urban challenge into a structured, buildable
technology specification — and then hands that spec to IBM Bob to actually build it."

**[0:35–1:45] — Walk the pipeline**
[Show: Evidence tab → Timeline tab → AI Analysis tab, moving briskly]

"It starts with evidence — photos, historical drainage maps, rainfall bulletins, citizen
reports — all placed on a real map with real coordinates.

Then AI analysis. This is the part I'm proudest of: it never blurs together what's actually
evidence, what's an AI interpretation, and what's simply unknown. A municipal team can trust
exactly how confident to be in each claim.

From there, CityMind proposes a solution — FloodWatch — and turns it into a full Solution
Blueprint: features, screens, user flows, and a complete database schema."

**[1:45–2:15] — The handoff**
[Show: Build with IBM Bob page, click Generate]

"And here's the handoff: CityMind generates a complete build prompt for IBM Bob — product
name, users, features, screens, data model, acceptance criteria, all of it."

**[2:15–4:15] — The main event: Bob building FloodWatch**
[Show: Bob IDE session — this should be your longest, highest-quality clip]

"I took that exact prompt into Bob and asked it to build FloodWatch. [Narrate what Bob is
doing as it happens — scaffolding the incident report form, wiring up the map, etc.] This
isn't a mockup of what Bob could do — this is Bob actually building it, from the spec
CityMind produced."

**[4:15–4:45] — The result**
[Show: FloodWatch running — incident report form, map with pins, dashboard]

"And here's FloodWatch running: a citizen can report an incident with a photo and location,
it shows up on the map, and a municipal team can verify it and track the response."

**[4:45–5:00] — Close**
[Show: CityMind + IBM Bob closing slide]

"CityMind structures the problem. Bob builds the solution. Together, that's a real path from
'this keeps happening' to a working civic-tech app — for Hyderabad, and for any city with a
recurring problem nobody's structured yet."

---

## Filming notes

- Section 2:15–4:15 (Bob actually building) is what judges are scoring — don't shortchange it
  in favor of more CityMind screen time.
- If Bob's build runs long, speed up the footage rather than cutting it — showing the real
  process (even sped up) is stronger than skipping straight to a finished result.
- Keep the CityMind sections brisk — they're context, not the point of the video.
